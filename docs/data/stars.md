## Files

### `stars.bin` + `stars.json`
Little-endian Float32, interleaved, stride 6 (24 bytes), `count` = 109,389 stars:
`[xPc, yPc, zPc, absMagV, colorIndexBV, teffK]`.
- x,y,z: heliocentric equatorial Cartesian, ICRS-aligned, J2000.0 epoch/equinox, parsecs, Sun at origin (+X to RA 0h, +Y to RA 6h, +Z to NCP). From HYG ra/dec/dist (dist = 1/parallax).
- absMagV: V - 5 log10(d/10 pc), no extinction.
- colorIndexBV: Johnson B-V (observed); **NaN** when unknown (1,540 stars). teffK: Ballesteros (2012) `T = 4600 (1/(0.92 BV+1.7) + 1/(0.92 BV+0.62))`, BV clamped to [-0.4, 2]; NaN when BV NaN. A rendering aid.
- Sorted by apparent V ascending (ties by HYG id) -> every prefix is a magnitude-limited subset (`magnitudeLimits` in header: e.g. V<6: 4,966; V<9: 78,161). Apparent mag = absMagV + 5 log10(|xyz|/10).
- Excluded (`excludedCount` 10,225): the Sun (1) and stars with HYG dist >= 100000 pc (no/negative parallax; 10,224). Positions/absMag are always finite.
- Header also holds `source`, provenance per field, `stats` (min/median/max distance), and `index i` rule: **byte offset = i*24**; `i` is the star index used everywhere below.

### `stars-named.json`
`{schemaVersion, generated, source, selection, fields, units, nullPolicy, selectionCounts, nameCrossCheck, count: 12355, rows}`; rows sorted by apparent mag.
`fields`: `index` (into stars.bin, **null** for 226 stars lacking distance), `hip`, `hd`, `hr`, `gl` (e.g. "Gl 244A"), `bayerFlamsteed` (display, e.g. "9 α CMa"), `properName`, `constellation` (IAU abbr), `spectralType`, `apparentMag` (V), `absMag`, `distPc`, `raDeg`, `decDeg` (J2000), `colorIndexBV`, `luminositySolar` (V-band), `variableName`, `hygId`, `properNameSource` ("iau-wgsn" 569 | "hyg" 41 | null). Missing = `null`.
Selection: proper name OR Bayer/Flamsteed OR Gliese id OR V<6.5. 610 stars have proper names (WGSN preferred; 70 of 640 WGSN entries are not in the HYG field).

### `hip-index.json`
`{"<HIP>": starsBinIndex}` for 107,737 stars with HIP numbers that are in `stars.bin`.

### `star-systems.json`
Multiple systems from HYG Gliese component fields (401): `fields` [`base`,`primaryHygId`,`distPc`,`members`], `memberFields` [`index`,`hygId`,`properName`,`gl`,`comp`,`apparentMag`]. E.g. Gl 559 = alpha Cen A/B at 1.3248 pc.

### `constellations.json`
`{source, conventions, lineStars, constellations[88]}`.
Per constellation: `abbr`, `name`, `genitive`, `englishName`, `lines` (array of `[hipA, hipB]` segments), `labelRaDeg/labelDecDeg` (+ `labelMethod`), `areaDeg2` (sums to 41253.0), `boundary` (array of rings; each ring = `[raDeg, decDeg]` J2000 vertices, implicitly closed, 1° sampling; Serpens has two rings, `boundaryParts` ["SER1","SER2"]; UMi and Octans rings encircle the poles), `boundaryParts`.
`lineStars`: `{fields:["hip","index","raDeg","decDeg","distPc","apparentMag"], rows}` for every HIP used by a line. `index` is **null** (and distPc null) for 19 endpoints without a usable parallax (draw them on the celestial sphere at a fixed distance); HIP 55203 (UMa) is not in HYG at all (all null).
**Perspective teaching**: resolve each endpoint through `hip-index.json` -> xyz, draw segments in 3-D; the figures distort as the camera leaves the Sun.

### `gaia-gcns.bin` + `gaia-gcns.json` (stretch)
Float32 LE, stride 5 (20 bytes), 330,881 stars within ~120 pc: `[xPc, yPc, zPc, absMagG, bpRp]` (ICRS equatorial, Sun at origin, **epoch J2016.0**), sorted by apparent G ascending. bpRp NaN for 6,574. 431 GCNS rows without G dropped. Distance = GCNS Bayesian median `dist_50` (archive gives kpc; converted to pc). Nearest: Proxima 1.302 pc.

### `manifest.part.json`
5 DatasetRecords (stars-hyg-field, stars-named-search, iau-wgsn-star-names, constellation-figures-boundaries, gaia-gcns-100pc).

## Processing notes
- Constellation boundaries: VI/49 `bound_20.dat` (J2000) is *not* polygon-ordered, so rings are rebuilt from `constbnd.dat` (B1875 polygons running along meridians/parallels), sampled every 1° in RA/Dec, precessed B1875.0 -> J2000 with IAU 1976 precession, then cross-checked: all 12,948 `bound_20.dat` vertices lie within 7.6″ of the rebuilt polylines; Orion area 594.1 deg² (IAU 594).
- Constellation lines: Stellarium `modern_iau` HIP pairs (deduplicated, undirected).
- GCNS: anonymous async TAP jobs are capped at 50,000 rows, so 24 RA bands of 15° are fetched and concatenated (331,312 rows total, matches table size).

## Validation (`validate.mjs`)
Counts; no NaN/Inf in xyz/absMag; apparent-magnitude monotone; Sirius d=2.637 pc, M_V=1.45, V=-1.44; Vega 7.68 pc, V=0.03; Betelgeuse 152.7 pc (Hipparcos 2007; literature 150–220), V=0.45; Polaris 132.6 pc, V=1.97; Proxima (HIP 70890) 1.296 pc; 88 constellations; area sum 41253.0; named stars 610. GCNS: nearest 1.302 pc (Proxima), BP-RP 3.80, M_G 13.41.

## Limitations
Hipparcos-era parallaxes (HYG): distances > ~300 pc uncertain (HYG carries no parallax errors); the 10,224 stars without parallax are absent from 3-D; no interstellar extinction; Teff is a colour-derived rendering value; constellation figures are one cultural convention; GCNS is licensed non-commercial.
